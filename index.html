Chief of Staff Confidential

The confidential truths behind the role.

Every executive has one. Almost nobody trains for the job.

Chief of Staff Confidential is a weekly interview podcast about the real work of chiefs of staff — the role behind the title. Each Monday, Maya asks the questions a chief of staff actually wrestles with, and Marcus answers from the chair: managing up, running the room, owning the calendar, coaching the principal, and making decisions land across an executive office.

No theory, no jargon. One question, one story, one idea you can use the same week — whether you're a first-time chief of staff or a seasoned one.

New episodes every Monday.
